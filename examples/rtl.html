ما الذي يُعكس؟ Spacing, text alignment and start/end positioning are written with logical properties, so they mirror automatically under dir="rtl". The raised and sunken bevels do not mirror: in the Win9x visual language the light source is fixed at the top-left, and Windows itself keeps it there in RTL. Flipping them would make every button read as sunken on the wrong edge.
